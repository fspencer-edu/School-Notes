# Overfitting and Underfitting

- Overfitting
	- Producing a model that performs well on the train data, but generalizes on the test data
- Underfitting
	- A model that does not perform on the training data

- Split data into 3
	- Training
	- Validation

# The Bias-Variance Tradeoff

- Both are measures of what would happen if you were to retrain the model many times on different sets of training data
- High bias and low variance correspond to underfitting
- Low bias, but high variance leads to overfitting
